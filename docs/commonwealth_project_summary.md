## 2. 100-Word Standard Application Statement

Climate volatility across Kenya's Arid and Semi-Arid Lands poses severe threats to pastoral livelihoods, while traditional humanitarian response suffers from 4-to-8-week assessment delays. To enable anticipatory action, I engineered AgriRisk Kenya—a machine learning decision-support architecture integrating CHIRPS satellite rainfall, MODIS vegetation health, WFP market prices, and spatial adjacency across 780 county-months.

Evaluated on held-out 2024 data, my calibrated ensemble achieved 83.3% recall in crisis anticipation (a 42.9% improvement over operational persistence baselines) and reduced probability prediction error by 70%. This work demonstrates how applied data science can empower institutions to mitigate food insecurity before acute malnutrition occurs.

## 3. 200-Word Detailed Research & Development Impact Essay

Across Kenya's Arid and Semi-Arid Lands, recurring drought and flash floods perpetuate acute food insecurity. Conventional humanitarian intervention is predominantly reactive: authoritative IPC consensus assessments require exhaustive household surveys taking 4 to 8 weeks, meaning relief mobilizes only after livestock herds perish and child malnutrition surges.

To bridge this operational latency gap, I developed AgriRisk Kenya, an empirical decision-support prototype designed to operationalize Forecast-based Financing and anticipatory action. The framework harmonizes 13 years of multi-sensor satellite earth observations (CHIRPS precipitation, MODIS 250m NDVI/VCI), WFP staple commodity price volatility, and first-order Queen contiguity spatial lag vectors. Using expanding-origin temporal backtesting with strict zero-leakage boundaries, my calibrated Random Forest model achieved 83.3% recall in predicting crisis conditions (IPC Phase 3+) 1 to 3 months ahead, outperforming standard operational persistence baselines by 42.9% and cutting probability prediction error by 70%.

This project underscores my commitment to advancing data-driven climate resilience in Sub-Saharan Africa. Through proposed postgraduate research, I aim to extend this methodology by integrating high-resolution Sentinel-2 optical imagery and spatio-temporal graph neural networks, directly supporting Kenya's Vision 2030 and UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 2: Zero Hunger; SDG 13: Climate Action).
