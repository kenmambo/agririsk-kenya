## 2. Three-Bullet Standard Version (Data Science & Analytics CV)

**AgriRisk Kenya — Climate & Food Security Early Warning System** *(2024)*
- **Data Engineering & Geospatial Integration:** Harmonized 13-year longitudinal panels (780 county-months) combining 0.05° CHIRPS precipitation, 250m MODIS vegetation indices, WFP cereal transaction dynamics, and 47-county Queen contiguity spatial spillover matrices with strict zero-leakage safeguards.
- **Predictive Modeling & Uncertainty:** Implemented expanding-origin temporal backtesting across 1–3 month forecasting horizons; Platt-calibrated Random Forest achieved **0.8333 Recall** (95% CI: 0.60–1.00) and **0.7692 F1**, outperforming the operational persistence baseline (0.5833 Recall) by 42.9% with a **70% reduction in Brier probability error** (0.1250 vs. 0.4167).
- **Decision-Support Software & Verification:** Productionized a multi-page Streamlit geospatial dashboard, FastAPI microservice, and reproducible experiment registry backed by 90 automated unit tests, comprehensive Model/Dataset Cards, and geographic equity audits.

## 3. Four-Bullet Technical Version (Machine Learning Engineering / Applied Research CV)

**AgriRisk Kenya — Multi-Source Geospatial ML Forecasting Architecture** *(Python 3.12, Scikit-Learn, GeoPandas)*
- **Multi-Modal Data Pipeline:** Architected automated, reproducible data ingestion pipelines extracting and validating satellite earth observations (CHIRPS pentad rainfall, MODIS 250m NDVI/VCI), WFP commodity market price momentum, and historical IPC acute food insecurity states across Kenya's Arid and Semi-Arid Lands (ASALs).
- **Spatial Feature Engineering:** Engineered 35 longitudinal predictors including standardized precipitation anomalies, price shock indicators, and first-order Queen contiguity spatial lag vectors capturing cross-county pastoral migration and market contagion.
- **Calibrated Multi-Horizon Forecasting:** Formulated expanding-origin temporal backtesting across 1-, 2-, and 3-month lead times; calibrated Random Forest ensembles achieved **0.8333 Recall** ($t+1$), **0.8333 Recall** ($t+2$), and **0.6667 Recall** ($t+3$) on held-out 2024 data, reducing Brier prediction error by 70.0% compared to standard persistence heuristics.
- **Rigorous Evaluation & MLOps:** Conducted domain ablation studies demonstrating critical reliance on prior IPC status (recall drops 0.90 to 0.20 when removed) and market signals (recall drops 0.90 to 0.70); packaged system with Platt probability calibration, block bootstrap 95% confidence intervals, Streamlit geospatial UI, FastAPI service, and a 90-test CI suite.
